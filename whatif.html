    How it is calculated

    • New units = units × (1 + elasticity × price change) × (1 + lift × marketing change)
    • Revenue = new units × new price
    • COGS = new units × new unit cost
    • Marketing and expenses scale directly with their % change
    • Profit = revenue − COGS − marketing − expenses
